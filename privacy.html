JARVIS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Overview

JARVIS is a private, self-hosted personal assistant operated for the owner's personal use. It accesses Google user data only to perform actions requested by the owner.

Google user data accessed

Depending on the requested task, JARVIS may access Gmail messages and metadata, Calendar events, Drive files and metadata, Docs, Sheets, Tasks, and Contacts. It may create, modify, send, or delete data when directed by the owner and authorized by the granted Google permissions.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to interpret and fulfil the owner's requests. Selected data may be transmitted to AI or API providers configured by the owner when necessary to perform those requests. JARVIS does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or make it publicly available.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's controlled device. Google user data may appear in local session history, operational logs, or task outputs as necessary to provide the service. Retention is controlled by the owner's local configuration and by the policies of any AI or API providers the owner chooses to use.

Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers selected by the owner when required to complete a requested task, or when required by law. It is not shared for independent marketing or advertising purposes.

Security

JARVIS uses OAuth rather than the owner's Google password. Access is limited to the permissions approved on Google's consent screen, and credentials are kept out of this public website and source repository.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke JARVIS access at any time from the Google Account connections page: myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the support address displayed on the JARVIS Google OAuth consent screen.
