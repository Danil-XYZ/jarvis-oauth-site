JARVIS

JARVIS is a private, self-hosted personal assistant used by its owner to work with the owner's own Google services.

Google services

The integration may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Tasks, and Contacts when the owner explicitly asks JARVIS to perform a task.

Personal use only

This application is not offered as a public service. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
